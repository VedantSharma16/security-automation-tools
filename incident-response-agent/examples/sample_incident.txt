Incident #4471 — EDR + SIEM correlation alert

A workstation in the finance VLAN (HOST-FIN-014) executed an obfuscated
PowerShell command shortly after a user reported receiving a suspicious
email attachment. Outbound HTTPS beacon traffic was observed to
45.155.205.28 at regular 60-second intervals, consistent with a C2
check-in pattern. Shortly after, EDR flagged a scheduled task created for
persistence, and a large archive was staged in a temp directory before an
outbound transfer to cdn-analytics-sync.net.

Separately, the vulnerability management team confirmed the host was still
unpatched for CVE-2021-44228 in a bundled logging library, and the
timeline suggests initial access may have exploited it via a
Log4Shell-vulnerable internal service reachable from the workstation's
subnet.
