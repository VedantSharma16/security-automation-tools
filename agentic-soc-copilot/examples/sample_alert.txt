EDR Alert #77021 - Suspicious process chain and outbound beaconing
Host: WKSTN-FIN-017
User: r.alvarez

Sysmon logged the following process creation on WKSTN-FIN-017:
  ParentImage: C:\Windows\explorer.exe
  Image: C:\Windows\System32\WindowsPowerShell\v1.0\powershell.exe
  CommandLine: powershell.exe -nop -w hidden -enc SQBFAFgAIAAoAE4AZQB3AC0ATwBiAGoA...

Shortly after, schtasks.exe was used to create a scheduled task named
"AdobeUpdateCheck" to re-run the same encoded command every 15 minutes,
establishing persistence on the host.

Outbound HTTPS traffic was then observed from the host to 91.203.145.12
on port 443, followed by periodic beacon-like check-ins to
secure-billing-portal.net roughly every 4 minutes.

The user reports opening an invoice attachment from an email that appeared
to come from a known vendor shortly before the activity began, consistent
with a phishing lure targeting the finance team.
